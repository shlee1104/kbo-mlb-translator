# Out-of-sample validation

Players posted from the KBO were removed from the fit entirely, then predicted. Every number below is out of sample.

## Fitted translations

`retention_slope` is what share of a player's edge over his own league survives the move. 1.0 means it carries across intact; 0 means the league erases it. `level_ratio` is the overall shift for a league-average player.
